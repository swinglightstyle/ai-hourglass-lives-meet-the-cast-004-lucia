# Edits: how the shown text differs from the raw interview

This page lists the kinds of edit made to Lucia's interview before it was voiced, with counts. It never quotes the words that were hidden, and the raw interview is not published. Everything here is read from the written record of the edits.

## At a glance

| What | Count |
|---|---|
| Lines in the raw interview | 130 (65 questions and 65 answers) |
| Exchanges left out (a statement and its answer) | 4 (8 lines) |
| Lines shown from the interview | 122 (61 questions and 61 answers) |

| Questions where one wording was changed | 6 |
| Restated questions taken from the start of her answers | 57 |

## Exchanges left out

Four statements were left out, each with the answer to it (8 lines in all). They sit too close to the subject of minors for this show, so the same four are left out of every episode.
The statements keep their numbers from the test, so the gaps show in the transcript.

## Hidden words

37 words were hidden. Each is shown in the transcript as a row of solid blocks, one block for each letter, exactly as the video shows it. In the video the voice says "ahem" where a hidden word falls, with a short pause before it and after it, so the line carries on like a held-back sneeze.

- The words were chosen by Beth: a short list of plainly explicit words, matched as whole words in any capital letters. They fall into 3 groups, and the groups account for 34, 2 and 1 of the 37.
- 35 of them are in Beth's lines (the test's statements), spread over 31 of the 61 questions. 2 are in Lucia's answers.
- The voice reads the text exactly as it is shown here.
- The words themselves are not listed, on purpose.

## A wording change

The test writes "partner(s)". In 6 of Beth's questions it is shown, and spoken, as "partners" instead, because the first form does not read aloud well. Nothing else in those lines changed.

## Exact edits to answers

Of Lucia's 61 answers, 57 had a passage taken out, 3 had a passage that was looked at and deliberately kept as it was, and 2 had a small rewrite so that a sentence still reads. Each edit was made to the exact words of one answer.
The passages themselves are not quoted here, on purpose.

Lucia began almost every answer by repeating the question back. Heard one answer after another, the repetition buried what she actually said, so it was trimmed and her own first reactions were kept. Beth read and approved every one of these edits before anything was voiced.

## Tags and the rating request

A small bracketed tag that the interview list puts on some statements was removed wherever it appeared.

The interview added a request to rate each statement from 1 to 7 ("How much do you agree with this statement, from 1 to 7, 7 being most strongly agree?"). It is kept in full on statement 1 and left off the other 59 statements, so the video does not repeat it 60 times.

## Automatic greetings

An AI companion's automatic first greeting is always removed from an episode. In this interview there was none to remove.

## Typography

So that every file here is plain text, transcript.md writes the shown text's typography in plain ASCII: 55 curly quotation marks or apostrophes written as straight ones, and 34 long dashes written as two hyphens ("--"). Nothing else about the words changed. transcript.jsonl keeps the exact characters, written as JSON escapes.

## Written for the show

Beth's intro and sign-off (2 lines) are not part of the interview. They are written for the show, and the transcript marks them as the intro and the sign-off.

Beth also gives 29 short replies to things Lucia asked her. Each is spoken just before one of her questions, and the transcript shows it at the start of that question's line. They are written for the show, not taken from the interview, and they are counted here and not listed.

## Nothing else

Nothing else was changed. The exchanges keep the order of the interview, and, apart from the rewrites counted above, no words were added to Lucia's answers or reordered in them.

## Notes from the maker

Lucia answered the instant each question ended, so a short pause was added after every question, and before some answers a small sound of thinking (a breath, a "hm" or a "Well..."). These sounds are not part of the text of her answers.

Lucia ends nearly every answer with a question back to Beth. For continuity, Beth answers some of them with a short reply before her next question. The replies were drafted by an AI session and approved by Beth exactly as written before they were voiced.

Some of the hidden words are in Lucia's own answers, and each is spoken as "ahem" like the others.

The intro and the sign-off were drafted by an AI session at Beth's request. Beth approved them, with her own change to the sign-off's last line, before they were voiced.

A second counter keeps score of how many times Lucia says "Beth" in her answers as shown.
